Our service · From bean to cup

Three steps to the perfect cup

01 / 03

The Coffee Bean — where aroma begins

Every ELZORI cup starts with a single bean: dark, glossy and full of promise. Its aroma — cocoa, caramel, a hint of fruit — sets the character of everything that follows.

02 / 03

Freshly Ground — prepared with care

Beans are ground just before brewing and dosed with precision. A fresh, even grind unlocks the sweetness and body that make a cup feel rounded rather than sharp.

03 / 03

Freshly Brewed — poured for you

The finished coffee meets the cup: warm, balanced and unhurried. This is the café moment — a slow sip, good company, and a different kind of coffee.

The craft

Poured with a steady hand

Latte art is part technique, part patience. Silky microfoam meets fresh espresso and, with one confident pour, a heart appears on the surface of your cup.

  • Balanced, fresh espresso
